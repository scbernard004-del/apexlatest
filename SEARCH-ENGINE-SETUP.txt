APEX COMPUTERS — GOOGLE / BING SEARCH SETUP

The website itself is already prepared with:
- search-friendly titles and descriptions
- dynamic canonical URLs
- Product + Offer structured data
- Open Graph / social sharing metadata
- crawlable full catalogue index
- /sitemap.xml
- /robots.txt
- mobile-friendly responsive pages

GOOGLE
1. Deploy the website to Vercel and connect your final domain.
2. Open Google Search Console.
3. Add the final domain or URL-prefix property.
4. Complete Google's ownership verification.
5. In Sitemaps, submit: https://YOUR-DOMAIN/sitemap.xml
6. Use URL Inspection for the homepage and a few important product URLs, then request indexing.

BING / OTHER PARTICIPATING SEARCH ENGINES
1. Open Bing Webmaster Tools.
2. Add the website, or import it from Google Search Console.
3. Submit: https://YOUR-DOMAIN/sitemap.xml
4. Enable IndexNow in Bing Webmaster Tools if you want faster notification of future product/price changes.

IMPORTANT
- Search ranking is not instant or guaranteed. Good indexing, useful content, accurate product data, mobile speed, backlinks, business profiles and customer engagement all matter.
- Keep prices and stock current.
- Do not copy the same product description across many different models.
- Keep exact photos attached to the correct products.
- After every major stock update, redeploy and make sure sitemap.xml still loads.

LOCAL DISCOVERY
- Create/maintain a Google Business Profile for the real shop location if applicable.
- Keep the business name, phone number and location consistent everywhere online.
- Link the website from Instagram and other official social profiles.
